I am very pleased to see the book [Citation Networks of European Constitutional Courts: Asymmetric Judicial Dialogues](https://doi.org/10.4324/9781032723914), edited by Lando Kirchmair and Lisa Lechner, published. The book is located at the intersection of constitutional law and political science and uses large-N textual data, text-as-data methods and network analysis to advance our understanding of citation practices among constitutional courts in Europe. It is the product of the [European Constitutional Court Network](https://eccn.at/) project, which I was fortunate to be part of during my Master's studies at the University of Innsbruck. I also contributed as a co-author to the chapter on the Austrian Constitutional Court.
