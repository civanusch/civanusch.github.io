This page provides an overview of all courses I have taught at the university level to date. My teaching covers both substantive topics, such as political communication and electoral analysis, and methodological topics, including data mining and computational text analysis. So far, I have taught courses at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich, the University of Innsbruck, and the Vienna School of International Studies. The individual courses are listed by year. By clicking on a course, you can access more detailed information about the course and its syllabus.
