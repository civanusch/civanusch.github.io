I am actively engaged in public outreach and science communication across a range of formats. My media activities so far include interviews and livestreams with ProSiebenSat.1 :newstime and Radio M94.5, as well as collaborations with newspapers and publishers through blog posts (see below).<br>
<br>
I am also committed to political education and science engagement in schools. I therefore serve as a volunteer [Science Ambassador](https://youngscience.oead.at/de/wissenschaftsvermittlung/wissenschaftsbotschafter-innen/botschafter-profile/657) and participate in [Wissenschaftswoche](https://www.bmb.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulpraxis/pwi/pa/wissenschaftswoche.html), organised by the Austrian Ministry of Education.
