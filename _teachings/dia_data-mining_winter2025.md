title: Data Mining (MSC Digital International Affairs)
description: "This course offers students a theoretical and practical introduction to data mining for the social sciences. With the transformative role of digitalisation in social life, vast amounts of observational data – such as behavioural, administrative, and textual data – are increasingly available for analysis. This development presents numerous opportunities, but also significant challenges for the social sciences. Traditional methods alone are often insufficient to make sense of such complex and high-volume data sets. Data mining equips social scientists with powerful tools to discover patterns, make predictions, and generate insights from unstructured or large-scale data. By learning these techniques, students will be better prepared to engage with contemporary research challenges and contribute to data-informed social inquiry."
